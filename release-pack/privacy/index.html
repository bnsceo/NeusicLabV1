Release Pack privacy notice

Last updated 16 November 2026

This explains what NeusicWave (“we”, “us”), run by Anderson Paulino as a sole owner, does with what you send through the Release Pack request form at neusicwave.com/release-pack/. Contact: hello@neusicwave.com.

Short version: we use what you send only to make your pack. We keep it until your project is done, then delete it, unless you ask us to keep it. We do not sell it, and this page has no ads. It counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies.

What we collect

Only what you type or send on the form:

  • your artist name and email;
  • the files you upload (tracks, stems, images, documents) and any link to your music;
  • facts for your bio, your links, and anything else you write;
  • whether the music was made in NeusicWave, which parts used AI, and whether you want the search presence (SEO) add-on;
  • your confirmation that you made the music and control the rights.

Nothing on this form takes payment. Like any website, the services that host this page and the upload service can see your IP address and browser details when you connect. We set no cookies. Visits are counted by Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies or browser storage and does not follow you from site to site. When you choose files to upload, a security check from Cloudflare runs (see below).

Why we use it

To make the artwork, bio + press kit and promo clips in the pack, and the search presence work if you ask for it; to write back to you; to keep a record of what you told us about rights and AI use, and to keep bots out of the upload.

Who handles it

  • Cloudflare runs the upload service and stores your uploaded files and details in private storage. Nothing is public. Cloudflare says it encrypts what it stores.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) passes the text of your request to our email inbox, and our email is hosted by Google. Their own terms apply to that step.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile checks that you are a person and not a bot when you choose files to upload. It runs in your browser and may store small pieces of data there to do its job. Cloudflare says the signals it uses are strictly necessary for detecting bots, and we do not turn on its clearance cookie. Cloudflare’s own Turnstile privacy terms apply.
  • GitHub hosts this web page.
  • AI tools. We use AI tools to build your artwork, bio, clips and search work. To do that, your material is sent to the tools we use, under those providers’ terms. A person reviews the results before anything is used.

We do not sell your information or share it with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your files and details until your project is done, then we delete them, unless you ask us to keep them (for example in a saved profile for your future work, which we would agree with you first). The copy of your request in our email inbox is deleted when we delete your files. Copies held in our providers’ own backups can take a little longer to disappear.

You can ask us to delete everything earlier at any time.

Your choices

Email hello@neusicwave.com from the address you used and ask to see what we hold, correct it, or delete it.

What you send stays yours

We claim no rights in your music or your material, and we use it only to make your pack. You tell us you made it and control the rights, so please only send material you have the right to share.

Security

Your files go to private storage over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and only we can open them. No system is perfectly secure, so send only what you are comfortable sharing.

Age

You must be at least 13. If you are under 18, a parent or guardian must agree and send it for you.

Changes

We may update this notice. The date at the top changes when we do. If a change matters to what you already sent us, we will tell you by email.
